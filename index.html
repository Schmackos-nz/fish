REELWILD

A little angler, a big wild valley. Wander the streams, rivers and lakes, read the water, and work every cast — open the bail, load the rod, spin the reel and fight what bites.

WASD walk   Shift run
Space open / close the bail
Hold LMB to load the cast, release on the peak
Space mid-flight to feather the line & stop short
Hold LMB + spin the mouse in circles to crank
RMB twitch / pop the lure
Space on the bite to set the hook
Fight: A/D side-strain, S drop rod on jumps
Q/E or wheel: drag down / up
F net the fish   R pick a wind knot
1–5 change lure   J journal
M map   E talk at the tackle shack
